Algorithm for quick sort:
Quick sort (A, LEFT, RIGHT)
Step 1:If LEFT < RIGHT go to step 2 to 7 Otherwise goto step 8
Step 2: Set Pivot=A[LEFT] Set i=LEFT Set j=RIGHT
Step 3:Repeat step 4 to 6 while i<j
Step 4:Repeat while A[i]<=pivot and i<=right Set i=i+1
[End of while]
Step 5:Repeat while A[j]>pivot Set j=j-1
[End of while]
Step 6: if i<j
Swap A[i] and A[j]
 [End of while]
Step 7:Swap A[j] and A[LEFT] 
Call quicksort(A,LEFT,j-1)
Call quicksort(A,j+1,RIGHT)
Step 8:Exit
